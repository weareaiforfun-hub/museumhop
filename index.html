MuseumHop: plan Museumnacht Amsterdam together

MuseumHop is a free planner for Museumnacht Amsterdam on Saturday 7 November 2026, from 19:00 to 02:00. Every activity, museum and food stop of the night is on one list and one map.

What you can do

  • Explore all 310 activities at 69 museums, filtered by type: music, talks, workshops, tours, film, performance, installations and more.
  • Pick your favourites and build your own route, or one shared route with your friends.
  • React to your friends' picks and see what the group wants to do.
  • See walking times between stops and the free buses and boats of the night.
  • Start from one of four suggested routes if you don't know where to begin.

How it works

No account needed: your name is remembered in this browser. Start a night and share its link; everyone with the link can view the plans and edit the shared route. Already in a night on another device? Get your link by email.
